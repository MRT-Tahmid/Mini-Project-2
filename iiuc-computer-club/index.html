A student community for people who build

IIUC Computer Club brings together students of International Islamic University Chittagong who are curious about technology. We learn by doing, and we help each other grow.

  • Building technical skills
  • Encouraging innovation
  • Promoting collaboration
  • Organizing technical workshops and competitions
  • Connecting students with opportunities in technology

What we do

Six areas where members learn, practice, and build together.

Competitive Programming

Practice algorithms and data structures, and prepare for contests with your peers.

Web Development

Learn to design and build responsive websites and web applications.

Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning

Explore how machines learn from data, from first models to real projects.

Cybersecurity

Understand how systems are attacked and how to protect them safely and ethically.

Robotics & Innovation

Turn ideas into working prototypes that combine hardware and software.

Workshops and Seminars

Hands-on sessions and talks that introduce new tools and career paths.
